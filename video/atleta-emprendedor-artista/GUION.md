# Atleta → Emprendedor → Artista — Video storytelling (motion graphics)

**Fuente:** reel de Instagram `DeiS9s1P2al` ("This is so niche that if you found this it was meant for you"), 98 s, en inglés, formato *talking head*.
**Resultado:** `atleta-emprendedor-artista.mp4` — 1080×1920 (9:16), 30 fps, 1 min 46 s. Solo motion graphics estilo póster editorial + banda sonora original sintetizada (100 BPM). Sin locución.

## 1. Transcripción original (EN)

> Hear me out, there's a certain type of person who will never be satisfied with doing just one thing with their life and they almost always follow a similar path. It usually starts with sports. You grow up playing some kind of sport and eventually you fall in love with your fitness. You start training, you push your limits, you start seeing what happens when you consistently put effort towards something and you get addicted to the feeling of progress. But eventually you start wondering, if I can completely transform my body through discipline and repetition, what else can I do it with? So you start getting interested in business. You read books, listen to podcasts, study successful people and you become obsessed with the idea of building something of your own. And you realize that same principle you learn through sports, discipline, competition, delayed gratification, they almost apply perfectly to entrepreneurship. But here's where things really get interesting because even though you love business and making money, there's another side to you that doesn't quite fit into either of those worlds. You love music, art, film, writing, design. You find yourself fascinated by philosophy, psychology, the way people think, you don't just wanna make money, you wanna create things. You wanna create something beautiful, experience everything life has to offer and understand as much of the world as possible. But after a while this is confusing because everyone keeps telling you to pick one thing but you can't imagine spending your entire life being just one thing. Eventually you realize maybe these interests aren't competing with each other but they actually complement each other. Maybe they're three parts of the same person. An athlete's body, an entrepreneur's mind, an artist's soul. And if you feel like you spent your entire life with too many interests to fit into one category, then you've just found your people.

## 4. Cómo se generó (sin créditos de Higgsfield)

Todo es código, renderizado en local:

- `src/timeline.json` — guion + tiempos + efectos de cada escena (edita aquí el texto o la duración).
- `src/index.html` — motor de motion graphics en Canvas: plantillas de póster, tipografía cinética, formas, grano.
- `src/render.mjs` — captura fotograma a fotograma con Chromium (Playwright) → ffmpeg.
- `src/score.py` — banda sonora y SFX sintetizados con numpy, sincronizados con `timeline.json`.
- Tipografías: Anton, Inter, Caveat, Great Vibes (OFL, vía Fontsource).

```bash
cd video/atleta-emprendedor-artista/src
node render.mjs frames /tmp/video_noaudio.mp4
python3 score.py /tmp/score.wav
ffmpeg -i /tmp/video_noaudio.mp4 -i /tmp/score.wav -c:v copy -c:a aac -b:a 192k -af loudnorm=I=-14:TP=-1.5 -shortest ../atleta-emprendedor-artista.mp4
```
