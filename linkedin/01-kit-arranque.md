### Post 1 · Semana 2, miércoles

**Balde:** Personal → Growth · **Formato:** Hot take · **CTA:** responder la pregunta

> I was president of a referral chapter in Tijuana for nine months. It showed me the ceiling faster than anything else could.
>
> Same room every week. Business owners passing each other names.
>
> It works. Beautifully. For a while.
>
> Then the room runs out of people. Not out of goodwill. Out of people. Everyone you could be introduced to, you already were.
>
> That's when most service companies find out they never built a second channel. Their entire pipeline is people remembering them.
>
> I see the same shape across the line. Device suppliers, contract manufacturers, RA/QA consultancies in San Diego and Orange County. Credentialed. Good at the work. Almost every client came from someone who already knew them.
>
> Referrals aren't a growth channel. They're a ceiling.
>
> One question to test it: if your three best referral sources retired tomorrow, where does next quarter's first conversation come from?
>
> If the answer took more than ten seconds, tell me what it was.

*~170 palabras. Hook = la línea más filosa del borrador, ya subida.*

### Post 3 · Semana 3, miércoles

**Balde:** Authority · **Formato:** Framework (ATE) · **CTA:** el ejercicio de las 10 consultas

> Most Class II device launches get funded on Attention and die in Treatment.
>
> Attention is how a buyer first hears about you. A trade show, a KOL, a distributor, a rep.
>
> Treatment is everything after. Who owns the inquiry. How fast someone answers. How an interested clinician becomes a pilot. Whether your CRM tells anyone what to do on Monday.
>
> Expansion is the second site, the second order, the system that hears about you from the first one.
>
> Most launch plans put the money into Attention. The reps get hired, the booth gets built, the deck gets polished.
>
> Then an inquiry comes in from a value analysis committee and nobody knows whose it is.
>
> 74% of medical device startups never return investor capital. The device usually works. The commercial system was the last line in the budget.
>
> Before your next rep hire, pull your last 10 inbound inquiries. For each one: who answered, how fast, and where is it today?
>
> If you can't answer that for 10, more Attention won't fix it.

*~185 palabras. El 74% lleva fuente en la estrategia (pág. 22): tenla a la mano por si alguien la pide en comentarios.*

## 4. Para prospectar, necesito de ti

Los DMs van uno por uno, con la observación específica de cada prospecto, nunca una plantilla con `[name]`. Desde aquí no puedo entrar a LinkedIn, así que necesito una de estas dos cosas:

1. **Ya tienes lista:** pega 10–20 prospectos (nombre, puesto, empresa, URL) y, si puedes, su último post o la vacante abierta. Te regreso la invitación + el DM del día 4 + el voice note del día 10 de cada uno.
2. **No tienes lista:** abre Sales Navigator con la búsqueda de cuentas de servicio (estrategia, pág. 10), califica 20 a mano con el escaneo de vacantes y pásame las que pasen.
